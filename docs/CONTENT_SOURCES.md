## General (non-company) information used in copy

These are general Finnish facts, not claims about WP-Putki. Review before production:

- Asbestos survey requirement: buildings whose construction began before 1994 need an asbestos survey before demolition/renovation work (Valtioneuvoston asetus asbestityön turvallisuudesta 798/2015).
- Typical asbestos locations (pipe insulation, tile joints/adhesives, fillers, paints) – general knowledge.
- District-heating substation service life "yleisen arvion mukaan noin 20–25 vuotta" (Energiateollisuus guidance) – presented as a general estimate.
- Kotitalousvähennys applies to the labour share of household maintenance and renovation work – the site links visitors to vero.fi for current terms (no amounts stated).

## Conflicts and items needing customer confirmation

1. **Main phone number:** the website uses 040 034 1377; Fonecta lists 010 320 5090 – confirm which one should be published.
2. **Opening hours:** website says 7.00–15.30; Fonecta lists 07:00–16:15. Demo uses the website.
3. **Store:** footer still says "myymälä 10.00–15.30" while /palvelut says sales only to account customers from 25.5. Demo says "Myymälä arkisin 10.00–15.30 (tiliasiakkaat)". Confirm hours and whether new account customers are accepted.
4. **"Työtilausten vastaanotto/myymälä" heading on /yhteystiedot** lists Arto Korhonen, then repeats him under "Urakointi ja tarjouslaskenta" – looks like a CMS leftover. Demo treats 040 034 1377 as the work-order line.
5. **Founding date / "yli 30 vuotta":** register says 7.12.1989. Confirm the company wants "vuodesta 1989" displayed.
6. **Customer feedback origin:** the quotes on the old site are anonymous. Confirm they are genuine customer comments (Google or other) before production.
7. **Google rating:** no rating could be verified (Google pages not machine-readable for this research); demo only links to Google Maps search for reviews. Production: a compliant Google Places integration or a manually confirmed rating.
8. **Asbestos removal permit:** asbestos removal requires an authorised contractor (AVI). If WP-Putki holds such a permit, it is a strong trust signal worth adding – not claimed in the demo.
9. **Service-manager role:** Yle (5.1.2024, https://yle.fi/a/74-20068034) quotes WP-Putki's huoltopäällikkö Ilkka Lihavainen about pipe-thawing jobs. He is not on the current contact page – not used. Pipe thawing ("jäätyneiden putkien sulatus") could be added as a winter service if confirmed.
10. **Facebook:** Vainu lists a Facebook channel, but no official page could be confirmed – no social links in the demo.
11. **Public procurement / references:** WP-Putki appears in search results with Joensuu-area TED notices, but awards could not be verified. No references or projects are shown; authentic case studies would be the biggest content upgrade.
12. **Employees / revenue:** Vainu estimates ~17 employees and €2.5 M revenue (2025) – estimates, not used.
